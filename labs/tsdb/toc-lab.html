The entry point

Why 52 bytes matter

The reader does not start at the top and scan downward. It seeks to the end of the file, reads the fixed-size TOC, and then jumps directly to the sections it needs.

Section detail

What you should learn here

The TOC is always at EOF. The read is based on fileSize - 52, not on a fixed absolute offset.
The TOC stores byte offsets. Those offsets act like jump targets for the rest of the file.
The offsets can point to empty compatibility slots. Current Prometheus still reserves deprecated slots even though query lookup uses the postings offset table path.
