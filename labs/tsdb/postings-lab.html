Label lookup

From label matcher to series IDs

If a query includes job="api-server", Prometheus needs a fast way to find every matching series. That fast way is a postings list: a sorted array of series IDs for one label pair.

Pick two postings lists and intersect them

Two-pointer merge trace

Why the lists are sorted

Sorted series IDs make intersection cheap. Prometheus can walk two arrays with two pointers instead of comparing every pair of entries.

Main mental model
Think of postings as an inverted index: label pair -> matching series IDs.

Sparse cache demo

Important rules to remember

Equal matchers do one direct postings lookup.
Regex matchers scan all values for that label name and union the postings for matching values.
Negation matchers are handled as subtraction from a positive working set.
